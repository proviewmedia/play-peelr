Frequently Asked Questions
Is Peelr free?
Yes. Peelr is completely free and always will be. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no hidden costs.
Does Peelr collect my data?
No. Peelr runs entirely on your device. We don't collect personal data, track your answers, or store anything on a server. Your language preference is saved locally on your device only.
How do I switch languages?
Open Settings from the footer menu, then select English or Spanish from the Language section.
Can I switch decks or layers during a game?
Yes. Tap the deck name or layer label at the top of the game screen to open the switcher.
How do I share a question card?
Tap the share icon at the bottom right of any question card. It will generate a shareable image you can send or post on social media.
Are new questions added?
Yes. We update the question library regularly. The app currently has 500 questions across 5 decks and 5 depth layers.
