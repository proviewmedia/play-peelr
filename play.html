Peelr — Conversation Card Game

500 Questions for Friends, Couples, Family, Coworkers & Strangers

Peelr is an online conversation card game with 500 handwritten questions across 5 decks and 5 depth layers. Layer 1 is free to try across all decks. Unlock the full game — Layers 2 through 5 plus monthly new questions — for $19.99 lifetime or $2.99 per month. No app download required.

Best Card Game for Couples and Date Night

The Lovers deck has 100 questions designed for couples, partners, and date nights. Start with fun surface-level questions and go all the way to soul-level conversations. Perfect for new relationships, long-term couples, and anyone who wants to connect deeper with their partner.

Family Game Night Questions

The Family deck brings 100 questions that help families talk about more than the usual stuff. Great for holidays, dinners, reunions, and road trips. Five depth layers let you choose how deep to go.

Icebreaker Questions for Friends

The Friends deck has 100 questions ranging from fun and lighthearted to deeply personal. Perfect for game nights, parties, camping trips, and getting to know your friends on a whole new level.

Team Building Game for Coworkers

The Coworkers deck has 100 questions designed for professional team building. Works for remote teams, office happy hours, retreats, and onboarding. Help your team connect beyond Slack messages and meetings.

Conversation Starters for Strangers

The Strangers deck has 100 questions for meeting new people. Perfect for parties, travel, networking events, and anyone who wants better conversations with people they just met.

How to Play Peelr

1. Pick a deck based on who you're playing with. 2. Choose a depth layer from 1 (surface) to 5 (soul level). 3. Take turns answering question cards. 4. Swipe for the next question. Available in English and Spanish. New questions added every month.

Privacy & Terms

Peelr is private by design. We use anonymous analytics to understand how people play, but we never track you personally, store your answers, or sell any data. Layer 1 is free to try; unlocking Layers 2–5 is a one-time $19.99 or $2.99/month.

PRICING

Layer 1 is free across all 5 decks — about 100 questions, no account needed. Unlocking Layers 2 through 5 (400+ deeper questions plus new ones every month) costs $19.99 one-time lifetime or $2.99 per month, your choice. Payments are processed by Stripe on the web and Apple on iOS. No hidden charges, no upsells, no autorenewal surprises.

ANONYMOUS ANALYTICS

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use Peelr — things like which decks are popular and how many people visit. This data is anonymous and aggregated. We don't collect your name, email, or any personal information. Your answers are never recorded, stored, or seen by anyone. Your conversations are yours.

NO DATA COLLECTION

Peelr runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Your language preference is saved locally on your device and never leaves it. If you clear your browser data, it's gone — because it was only ever yours.

HOW PEELR MAKES MONEY

Peelr Premium ($19.99 lifetime or $2.99/month) is the primary way Peelr supports itself. Sponsored cards shown to free users contain affiliate links — if you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Premium users do not see sponsored cards. No ads, no data selling, no hidden monetization.

TRANSPARENCY

Peelr is a single HTML file. There's no hidden backend, no secret data pipeline, no third-party SDKs. What you see is what there is. You can inspect the source code yourself — it's all right there.

TERMS OF USE

Peelr is provided as-is for personal, non-commercial use. The questions are original and belong to Peelr. Don't copy the questions and pretend they're yours — that's not cool. Play the game, share the game, enjoy the game. That's all we ask.

Your conversations are sacred. We built Peelr to protect that.
