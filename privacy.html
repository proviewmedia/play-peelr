No Data Collection

Peelr runs entirely in your browser or on your device. Your answers are never recorded, stored, or seen by anyone. Your language preference is saved locally on your device and never leaves it. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information.

Anonymous Analytics

We use anonymous analytics to understand how people use Peelr — things like which decks are popular and how many people play. This data is completely anonymous and aggregated. It cannot be used to identify you.
