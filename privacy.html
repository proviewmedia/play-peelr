Privacy & Terms

Peelr is free, private, and always will be. We use anonymous analytics to understand how people play, but we never track you personally, store your answers, or sell any data.

No Data Collection

Peelr runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Your language preference is saved locally on your device and never leaves it. If you clear your browser data, it's gone because it was only ever yours.

How Peelr Makes Money

The sponsored cards you see between questions contain affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's the entire business model. No ads, no data selling, no hidden monetization. Just affiliate links and the occasional coffee from kind people.

Transparency

Peelr is a single HTML file. There's no hidden backend, no secret data pipeline, no third-party SDKs. What you see is what there is. You can inspect the source code yourself. It's all right there.

Terms of Use

Peelr is provided as-is for personal, non-commercial use. The questions are original and belong to Peelr. Don't copy the questions and pretend they're yours. Play the game, share the game, enjoy the game. That's all we ask.

Your conversations are sacred. We built Peelr to protect that.
