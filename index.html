The Peel

THE STORY

"I started Peelr for myself."

I'm a designer, and like a lot of designers, I notice the gaps between how things feel and how they could feel. One of those gaps was in conversation.

I used to carry a physical deck of question cards everywhere — to dinners with friends, to family gatherings, on trips. The cards had this way of cracking things open. A question would land and suddenly we weren't talking about work or the weather anymore. We were somewhere real.

Then one day I lost the deck. I looked everywhere. Couldn't find it. And I realized — I'd been dependent on a physical object to have the conversations I actually wanted to have. That felt like a problem worth solving.

So I built Peelr. A digital deck that lives in your pocket, works anywhere, never gets lost, and never runs out of questions.

The second reason was travel. I move around a lot, and one of my favorite things about being somewhere new is the strangers. The person next to you on a flight, the host you're staying with, the someone you end up talking to at 2am in a city you don't live in. Those conversations can be extraordinary — if you ask the right thing.

At its core, this is a tool for going deeper with the people in front of you — whoever they are. Friends you've known for years but never really asked. Family members whose stories you've never heard. A partner you love but still want to know more of. A stranger you only have one night with.

Every question is written with care. New ones arrive every month.

If a draw ever took you somewhere you didn't expect — I'd genuinely love to hear about it.

Privacy & Legal

Plain language. No fine print.
THE SHORT VERSION

Peelr is a free card game. We don't collect your personal data. We don't track you. We don't sell anything about you to anyone. You don't need an account to play. You're just here to have better conversations — and so are we.

DATA & PRIVACY

Peelr does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any identifying information to use the app. We do not track your location. We do not use cookies to follow you across the internet. We do not sell your data to third parties. Ever.

The app may use basic anonymous analytics to help us improve the experience. This data is aggregate and anonymous — it cannot be used to identify you individually.

Your conversations are your own. What happens in a Peel stays in a Peel. We have no access to what you say, share, or discuss while playing.

SPONSORED MOMENTS & AFFILIATE LINKS

Peelr is free and will always be free. To support the ongoing development of the app, we occasionally include sponsored moments between draws. These are branded cards that appear between question cards during a session. They are clearly labeled "Sponsored Moment." You can swipe past any sponsored card instantly.

Some sponsored cards may contain affiliate links. If you tap through and make a purchase, Peelr may receive a small commission at no cost to you. Sponsors do not have access to any of your data.

ADULT CONTENT

Peelr contains questions intended for mature audiences, particularly within the Lovers deck at deeper layers. Some questions are explicitly sexual or deeply intimate. These questions are designed for consenting adults in established relationships.

By choosing the Lovers deck at Layer 4 or Layer 5, you confirm that you are 18 years of age or older and that all participants in your session are consenting adults.

Peelr is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. If you are under 18, please use the Friends, Family, Coworkers, or Strangers decks at Layers 1 through 3.

How to Peel

STEP 01

Pick Your People
Choose who you're playing with — Friends, Family, Coworkers, Lovers, or Strangers.

STEP 02

Choose Your Layer
Select a depth from Layer 1 (light and fun) to Layer 5 (soul level). You decide how deep.

STEP 03

Pull a Draw and Answer
Swipe through questions one at a time. Read each one out loud. Everyone answers. There are no wrong answers.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

• You can switch decks or layers by tapping the dots on any card
• Questions cycle back around when you reach the end
• There's no winning. There's only going deeper.
