FAQ

All you need to know.

How do I play Peelr?

Pick a deck based on who you are with, choose a depth layer from 1 to 5, and start swiping through questions. Take turns answering out loud. No accounts, no logins, no setup required.

Is Peelr really free?

Yes. Layers 1 through 3 are completely free across all 5 decks — over 300 questions with no paywall. Layers 4 and 5 are available through a $1.99/month Peelr Premium subscription on the iOS app.

Can I use Peelr for team building, HR events, or offsites?

Absolutely. The Coworkers deck is built specifically for professional settings — questions that create genuine connection without crossing personal boundaries. Layer 1 and 2 work great for any group size and make excellent icebreakers for new team onboarding, offsites, and HR facilitated events.

How is Peelr different from We're Not Really Strangers or TableTopics?

Peelr adapts to who you are with. You pick a deck for your specific relationship and a layer for how deep you want to go. It is free to start, always in your pocket, and grows with new questions every month. No physical cards to lose or run out of.

Do questions ever repeat?

New questions are added every month automatically. Your deck keeps growing without any app update required.

Can I play on my computer or any device?

Yes. Visit playpeelr.com/play to play free in your browser on any device. The iOS app adds premium features and the $1.99/month subscription for Layers 4 and 5.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. All 500+ questions are available in English and Spanish. Toggle the language in settings.

What is a good conversation card game for couples?

Peelr's Lovers deck is designed specifically for couples — 100 questions from playful to deeply vulnerable. Layer 5, Soul Ties, has the kind of questions that stay with you long after the game ends.
