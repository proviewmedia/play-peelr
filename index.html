The Peel

THE STORY

"I started Peelr for myself."

I'm a designer, and like a lot of designers, I notice the gaps between how things feel and how they could feel. One of those gaps was in conversation.

I used to carry a physical deck of question cards everywhere — to dinners with friends, to family gatherings, on trips. The cards had this way of cracking things open. A question would land and suddenly we weren't talking about work or the weather anymore. We were somewhere real.

Then one day I lost the deck. I looked everywhere. Couldn't find it. And I realized — I'd been dependent on a physical object to have the conversations I actually wanted to have. That felt like a problem worth solving.

So I built Peelr. A digital deck that lives in your pocket, works anywhere, never gets lost, and never runs out of questions.

The second reason was travel. I move around a lot, and one of my favorite things about being somewhere new is the strangers. The person next to you on a flight, the host you're staying with, the someone you end up talking to at 2am in a city you don't live in. Those conversations can be extraordinary — if you ask the right thing.

At its core, this is a tool for going deeper with the people in front of you — whoever they are. Friends you've known for years but never really asked. Family members whose stories you've never heard. A partner you love but still want to know more of. A stranger you only have one night with.

Every question is written with care. New ones arrive every month.

If a draw ever took you somewhere you didn't expect — I'd genuinely love to hear about it.

How to Peel

STEP 01

Pick Your People
Choose who you're playing with — Friends, Family, Coworkers, Lovers, or Strangers.

STEP 02

Choose Your Layer
Select a depth from Layer 1 (light and fun) to Layer 5 (soul level). You decide how deep.

STEP 03

Pull a Draw and Answer
Swipe through questions one at a time. Read each one out loud. Everyone answers. There are no wrong answers.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

• You can switch decks or layers by tapping the menu icon (⋮) on any card

• Use swipe gestures or arrow buttons to navigate

• There's no winning. There's only going deeper.
