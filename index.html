FAQ

All you need to know.

How do I play Peelr?

Pick a deck based on who you're with, choose a depth layer from 1 to 5, and start swiping through questions. Take turns answering out loud. No accounts, no logins, no setup required.

Is Peelr really free?

Layer 1 is free across all 5 decks — about 100 questions, no account needed. Unlocking Layers 2 through 5 (400+ deeper questions plus a new drop every month) is $19.99 one-time lifetime, or $2.99/month if you'd rather subscribe. Both work on web and iOS.

How is Peelr different from We're Not Really Strangers or TableTopics?

Peelr adapts to who you're with. You pick a deck for your specific relationship and a layer for how deep you want to go. Layer 1 is free to try, premium unlocks the rest, and the game grows with new questions every month. No physical cards to lose or run out of.

Can I use Peelr for team building or offsites?

Absolutely. The Coworkers deck is built specifically for professional settings — questions that create genuine connection without crossing personal boundaries. Layers 1 and 2 make excellent icebreakers for onboarding, offsites, and HR-facilitated events.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. All 500+ questions are available in English and Spanish. Toggle the language in settings.

Can I play on my computer or any device?

Yes. Visit playpeelr.com/play to try Layer 1 in your browser on any device, or download the iOS app. Unlock the full game with $19.99 lifetime or $2.99/mo — same access on web and iOS.
