The Peel

THE STORY

"It started with a deck of cards I couldn't find."

I used to carry a physical deck of question cards with me everywhere. Dinners, road trips, holidays — I'd pull it out and something would shift. Someone would answer a question and the whole table would go quiet. Not awkward quiet. The kind of quiet where everyone's actually listening. Those were always the best nights.

Then one night I reached for the deck and it wasn't there. I'd left it somewhere, or lost it — I still don't know. And the conversation that night? It was fine. Normal. Surface-level. I remember thinking: we were all sitting right next to each other, but nobody was really talking.

That's when it hit me. I shouldn't need a physical deck to have the conversations that actually matter. It should always be in my pocket. Ready whenever the moment is right.

Once I started building it, I realized the need was everywhere. My friends and I had known each other for years, but there were things we'd never asked. My family would get together for holidays and talk about the same things every time — never the real stuff, never the things that actually bring you closer.

Then there was work. I work remotely, and when your team is scattered across screens, it's easy to stay transactional. Meetings start and end. You never learn who someone actually is. I wanted something that could change that — even over a video call.

And then there were the strangers. I travel a lot, and some of the most meaningful conversations I've ever had were with people I'll probably never see again. The person next to you on a flight. Someone you meet at 2am in a city you don't live in. Those moments deserve better than small talk.

So that's what Peelr is. A way to ask deeper, more meaningful questions — as a group, as a couple, or with someone you just met. No app to download. No account to create. Just open it, pick your people, choose how deep, and start peeling.

Every question is written with care. New ones arrive every month.

If a question ever took you somewhere you didn't expect — I'd love to hear about it.

How to Peel

STEP 01

Pick Your People
Choose who you're playing with — Friends, Family, Coworkers, Lovers, or Strangers.

STEP 02

Choose Your Layer
Select a depth from Layer 1 (light and fun) to Layer 5 (soul level). You decide how deep.

STEP 03

Pull a Draw and Answer
Swipe through questions one at a time. Read each one out loud. Everyone answers. There are no wrong answers.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

• You can switch decks or layers by tapping the menu icon (⋮) on any card

• Use swipe gestures or arrow buttons to navigate

• There's no winning. There's only going deeper.

Privacy & Terms

Peelr is free, private, and always will be. We use anonymous analytics to understand how people play, but we never track you personally, store your answers, or sell any data. The only links that make us money are clearly marked affiliate links.

FREE FOREVER

Peelr is completely free to use. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no premium tiers. Every question, every deck, every layer — free. That's a promise.

ANONYMOUS ANALYTICS

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use Peelr — things like which decks are popular and how many people visit. This data is anonymous and aggregated. We don't collect your name, email, or any personal information. Your answers are never recorded, stored, or seen by anyone. Your conversations are yours.

NO DATA COLLECTION

Peelr runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Your language preference is saved locally on your device and never leaves it. If you clear your browser data, it's gone — because it was only ever yours.

HOW PEELR MAKES MONEY

The sponsored cards you see between questions contain affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. That's it. That's the entire business model. No ads, no data selling, no hidden monetization. Just affiliate links and the occasional coffee from kind people.

TRANSPARENCY

Peelr is a single HTML file. There's no hidden backend, no secret data pipeline, no third-party SDKs. What you see is what there is. You can inspect the source code yourself — it's all right there.

TERMS OF USE

Peelr is provided as-is for personal, non-commercial use. The questions are original and belong to Peelr. Don't copy the questions and pretend they're yours — that's not cool. Play the game, share the game, enjoy the game. That's all we ask.

Your conversations are sacred. We built Peelr to protect that.
