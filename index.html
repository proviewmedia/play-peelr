FAQ

All you need to know.

How do I play Peelr?

Pick a deck based on who you are with, choose a depth layer (1 to 5), and start swiping through questions. Take turns answering out loud. No accounts, no logins, no setup required.

Is Peelr really free?

Yes. Layers 1 through 3 are completely free across all 5 decks. That is over 300 questions with no paywall. Layers 4 and 5 are available through a $1.99/month Peelr Premium subscription on the iOS app.

How is Peelr different from We're Not Really Strangers or TableTopics?

Peelr adapts to who you are with. You pick a deck for your specific relationship and a layer for how deep you want to go. It is free to start, always in your pocket, and grows with new questions every month. No physical cards to lose or run out of.

Do questions ever repeat?

New questions are added every month automatically. Your deck keeps growing without any app update required.

Can I play on my computer or any device?

Yes. Visit playpeelr.com/play to play in your browser on any device. The iOS app adds premium features, offline access, and the $1.99/month subscription for Layers 4 and 5.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. All 500+ questions are available in both English and Spanish. Toggle the language in settings.

What is a good conversation card game for couples?

Peelr's Lovers deck is designed specifically for couples, with 100 questions ranging from playful to deeply vulnerable. Layer 5 questions are the kind you will talk about long after the game ends.

Can I use Peelr as an icebreaker at work or a party?

Absolutely. The Coworkers deck keeps questions professional and fun. The Strangers deck is perfect for parties and meeting new people. Layer 1 and 2 are great icebreaker question levels for any group setting.
